# Proof Artifact — Sample SaaS Onboarding Plan

Purpose: show implementation thinking for Karbon without claiming prior Karbon, accounting-firm, or B2B SaaS implementation employment.

## Scenario

A small firm is adopting a new workflow platform. The goal is to help the team move from scattered tasks and unclear ownership into a repeatable workflow.

## Phase 1 — Kickoff

- Confirm goals.
- Identify admin and key users.
- Define success criteria.
- Clarify timeline and responsibilities.
- Send recap with next steps.

## Phase 2 — Configuration

- Map customer needs to a simple starting workflow.
- Confirm user roles and setup decisions.
- Track open questions.
- Avoid over-customizing before launch.

## Phase 3 — Data Check

- Review contacts or work items in a spreadsheet.
- Look for missing fields, duplicates, outdated records, and inconsistent naming.
- Confirm accuracy before import or launch.

## Phase 4 — Training

- Explain what changes and why it matters.
- Walk users through the daily workflow.
- Create a simple FAQ for common questions.
- Note adoption concerns and blockers.

## Phase 5 — Go-Live and Handoff

- Confirm the workflow is usable.
- Track early wins.
- Document open issues.
- Prepare handoff notes for support or customer success.
