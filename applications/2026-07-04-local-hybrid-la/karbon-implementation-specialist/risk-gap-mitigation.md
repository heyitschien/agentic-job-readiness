# Risk & Gap Mitigation — Karbon Implementation Specialist

## Verdict

Apply as a stretch role if the application does not hard-block on 2–4+ years of B2B SaaS implementation experience.

## Main gaps

- No formal B2B SaaS Implementation Specialist title.
- No accounting or tax firm employment.
- No direct Karbon platform experience.
- No claim of owning enterprise data migrations.

## Mitigation strategy

Use a direct, credible positioning:

> I have not held a formal B2B SaaS Implementation Specialist title. I do bring years of customer-facing ownership, training/documentation proof, milestone-style follow-through, AI-assisted workflow habits, and intentional focus on onboarding and implementation as my next role.

## What to emphasize

- Customer-facing ownership.
- Organized onboarding follow-through.
- Clear communication and expectation-setting.
- Training and walkthrough ability.
- Documentation and handoff notes.
- Spreadsheets and data accuracy mindset.
- AI-assisted project planning and communication.
- Change-management mindset from customer operations.
- Remote readiness from Pacific Time.

## What not to overclaim

- Do not claim 2–4+ years B2B SaaS implementation if untrue.
- Do not invent accounting or tax firm domain experience.
- Do not invent end-to-end data migration ownership.
- Do not invent Salesforce, HubSpot, or Karbon administration experience.
- Do not position as a software engineer or solutions architect.

## Screening watch

Stop and report if the application form requires a mandatory checkbox for 2+ years of B2B SaaS implementation or accounting industry experience with no room for explanation.

## Readiness label

Stretch but worthwhile. Estimated readiness: 70–72 percent.
